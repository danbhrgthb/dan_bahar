### MEGAHIT

MEGAHIT is an ultra-fast and memory-efficient Next Generation Sequencing assembler. It is optimized for metagenomes, but also works well on generic single genome assembly (small or mammalian size) and single-cell assembly.
In short, MEGAHIT organizes shorter DNA sequencing reads together into longer sequences, which are called contigs.

micromamba create -n megahit_env megahit
micromamba activate megahit_env
megahit --version
